12/09/2026 Mbarara Fuel & Client meeting UGX 500,000 UGX 80,000

Current-State Characteristics & Bottlenecks

  • • Expense management is entirely manual.
  • • Expense requests are handled verbally or informally.
  • • Approval is performed directly by the boss.
  • • Money is issued in cash after approval.
  • • Expenses are recorded manually on paper logs.
  • • The record contains extremely limited contextual info.
  • • Receipts are currently optional / frequently lost.
  • • Additional funds can be requested when cash runs low.
  • • Employees use personal money when advances fall short.
  • • Employees subsequently file for reimbursement manually.
  • • Balance reconciliation is done manually on scrap paper.
  • • Reports require tedious consolidation of handwritten notes.
  • • Complete absence of a centralized digital audit trail.

3. Five Core Financial Concepts

The domain model pillars governing all system transactions and accounting rules

A
Expense Request / Advance

The upfront company fund allocation requested prior to an operational task.

“I need UGX 200,000 from the company for travel.”
B
Actual Expense

The Total cost incurred during the task against the issued advance.

“I actually spent UGX 150,000 on fuel and food.”
C
Additional Advance

Supplementary company funds requested when unforeseen field costs arise.

“The original UGX 200,000 isn't enough. I need another UGX 50,000.”
D
Employee Reimbursement

Personal funds spent on behalf of the company due to insufficient cash advances.

“I spent UGX 30,000 of my own money. The company owes me UGX 30,000.”
E
Reconciliation & Settlement

The mathematical settlement determining the final financial outcome of an advance:

“Let's determine exactly what happened to the money: Surplus returned to company, or Shortfall reimbursed to employee.”

4. Core Business Rules (BR-001 – BR-008)

Inviolable business logic and operational constraints enforced by the system

Advance Authorization Requirement BR-001
An expense request must be approved by the designated approver before company funds can be physically or electronically issued.
Mandatory Rejection Justification BR-002
Any rejected request or expense line item must contain an explicit reason entered by the approver for auditing and employee feedback.
Traceable Expense Association BR-003
An employee must be able to record actual expenses against an approved advance, maintaining strict relational binding between disbursement and spending.
Flexible Receipt Attachment Policy BR-004
Receipts are optional for small incidental company expenses (e.g., boda-boda, parking, tolls), but strongly recommended when formal vouchers exist.
Supplemental Advance Authorization BR-005
An employee may request additional funds when the original advance is insufficient; this supplemental request requires independent review and approval.
Permitted Personal Out-of-Pocket Expenditure BR-006
An employee may legitimately use personal funds when company cash is insufficient or unavailable during field operations.
Reimbursement Eligibility & Claim BR-007
Personal expenditure incurred on behalf of the company may be submitted for formal reimbursement subject to management review and sign-off.
Deterministic Reconciliation Outcome BR-008
The system must deterministically calculate the final financial balance of an advance (Total Issued − Total Spent) with zero unallocated floating funds.

5. Functional Requirements (FR-01 – FR-08)

Detailed specifications of what the software application must execute

FR-01 — User Authentication & Access Control
FR-01

The system shall:

  • Allow authorized users to log in securely with email/password .
  • Identify and track the authenticated user across all sessions.
  • Enforce Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) restricting employees to their own claims and approvers to managerial functions.
  • Allow system administrators to provision, edit, and deactivate user accounts.
FR-02 — Expense Request / Advance Workflow
FR-02
Employee Functionality:
  • Create an expense advance request.
  • Specify requested amount (in UGX).
  • Specify destination (e.g., Mbarara, Gulu, Kampala).
  • Specify business reason and operational purpose.
  • Submit request and track approval status in real time.
Approver Functionality:
  • View pending queue of submitted requests.
  • Approve advance requests with 1-click.
  • Reject requests with mandatory justification text.
  • Audit full request history and timestamp logs.
System Records: Requester ID, Requested Amount, Date/Time, Destination, Reason, Current Status, Approver ID, Approval/Rejection Timestamp, and Rejection Reason.
FR-03 — Funds Issuance & Custody
FR-03

After an advance is approved, the system shall allow the authorized custodian to log the physical or electronic release of capital:

  • Amount physically issued (which may match or be adjusted from requested amount).
  • Date and time of issuance.
  • Recipient identification and acknowledgment signature/flag.
  • Disbursing officer / cash custodian identity.
  • Link to the original advance request record.
FR-04 — Expense Recording (Field & Office)
FR-04

The system shall allow an employee to log actual expenses associated with an active approved advance:

  • Date of expenditure.
  • Destination / site location.
  • Expense Category (Fuel, Food, Transport, Accommodation, Parking, Misc).
  • Detailed description and purpose of the purchase.
  • Actual amount spent.
  • Ability to record expenses either while on the road (mobile) or after returning to the office.
FR-05 — Receipt & Voucher Attachment
FR-05

The system shall allow the employee to attach digital receipt files (camera photos from mobile or image uploads from desktop) to any expense line item:

  • Support mobile camera capture with automatic image compression.
  • Support JPG, PNG, and PDF file formats.
  • Maintain receipt availability even if receipts are marked optional under BR-004.
FR-06 — Supplemental / Additional Advance
FR-06

When original funds are insufficient during field activities, the system shall permit requesting additional capital:

  • Explicitly links to the existing active advance task.
  • Specifies additional amount required and emergency justification.
  • Routes through manager approval before release.
  • Automatically merges approved supplemental funds into the master advance balance calculation.
FR-07 — Out-of-Pocket Reimbursements
FR-07

The system shall allow an employee to claim expenses paid out of personal pocket for legitimate company activities:

Pending Approved Rejected Paid

The system tracks reimbursement status through a formal lifecycle until the employee is repaid.

FR-08 — Managerial Expense Review
FR-08

The authorized approver shall inspect submitted expense line items:

  • Inspect itemized cost breakdowns, descriptions, and categories.
  • Zoom and view attached receipt photos.
  • Approve legitimate expenses or reject invalid items with mandatory feedback reasons.

6. Reconciliation Engine (FR-09)

Deterministic mathematical reconciliation models with concrete enterprise scenarios

The system calculates the final financial position comparing Total Funds Issued vs. Total Legitimate Expenses, incorporating supplemental advances and personal reimbursements.

Scenario A: Cash Surplus Balance Return

Employee spends less than the total company funds disbursed.

Initial Advance: UGX 500,000
Additional Advance: + UGX 50,000
Total Company Funds: UGX 550,000
Actual Approved Expenses: − UGX 450,000
Net Balance Surrendered: UGX 100,000
✅ UGX 100,000 must be returned to the company safe.
Scenario B: Cash Shortfall Reimbursement

Employee spends more than the initial advance using out-of-pocket funds.

Initial Advance: UGX 500,000
Actual Approved Expenses: − UGX 550,000
Shortfall Deficit: UGX 50,000
⚠️ UGX 50,000 is owed and reimbursed to the employee.

8. Policy Decisions & Business Clarifications

Concrete architectural decisions resolved during stakeholder requirement interviews

Taxonomy
What expense categories are officially supported?
6 Standard Categories
Fuel, Food/Meals, Transport, Accommodation, Parking, and Miscellaneous.
Concurrency
Can one employee hold two active advances simultaneously?
YES • Supported
System must distinctly isolate and track each advance without merging balances.
Authorization
Is every personal expense automatically reimbursable?
NO • Managerial Discretion
The boss must review and explicitly approve all out-of-pocket claims.
Governance
Does an additional advance require a new approval?
YES • Mandatory Sign-off
Every supplementary cash disbursement requires fresh managerial approval.
Custody
How is surplus money returned by the employee recorded?
Direct System Entry
Custodian confirms physical cash receipt inside the reconciliation screen.
Self-Service
Can an employee cancel an unapproved advance request?
YES • Permitted
Employees can cancel requests as long as the status remains in 'Pending'.
Modification
Can an employee edit an expense after submitting it?
YES • Prior to Approval
Editable while pending review; changes trigger updated audit timestamps.
Correction Window
Can a rejected expense be corrected and resubmitted?
YES • 3-Day Strict Limit
Resubmissions must occur within 3 calendar days of rejection notice.
Audit Security
Can anyone modify an already reconciled and closed expense?
STRICTLY NO • Immutable
Once an advance is finalized and reconciled, records become permanently read-only for all roles to guarantee audit integrity.

9. Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)

System quality attributes, performance SLAs, security constraints, and mobile usability

⚡
a. Performance
Standard queries, expense lookups, and submissions shall respond in < 1.5 seconds under expected concurrent office and field load.
🕒
b. Availability
99.5% uptime during defined corporate business hours (7:00 AM – 8:00 PM EAT, Monday to Saturday).
🔒
c. Security & RBAC
Strict role-based isolation. Employees cannot view peer advances; approvers cannot approve their own expense requests (separation of duties).
🔑
d. Authentication
Cryptographically salted password hashing with secure JWT / session tokens and automatic idle session timeouts.
📜
e. Auditability
Every financial transaction and status transition must write an immutable audit log entry capturing user, timestamp, IP, and changes.
🛡️
f. Reliability & Integrity
ACID database transactions. Zero partial writes; expense submission cannot fail halfway leaving orphaned receipts or corrupt figures.
💾
g. Backup Strategy
Nightly automated database dumps replicated off-site with 30-day point-in-time retention and monthly restoration drill verification.
🎯
h. Usability
Intuitive, frictionless mobile interface allowing field workers to submit an expense in under 45 seconds with zero formal technical training.
💻
i. Cross-Platform Compatibility
Seamless rendering across Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Safari; responsive support for .NET MAUI Blazor Hybrid mobile views.
📱
Mobile Field Optimization
Must operate reliably on budget Android smartphones over intermittent 3G/4G cellular networks, utilizing client-side image compression before upload.
